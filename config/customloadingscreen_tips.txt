Use the JEI search bar to look up recipes and item uses before asking in Discord.
The quest book guides your progression. Press the quest key to open it.
Machines produce visible pollution. Plan your factory layout to keep pollution away from your base.
Never explore without armor, weapons, and a way home.
You cannot craft a Nether Portal in this pack. Dig down to reach the Nether instead.
Dig to bedrock to access The Beneath dimension.
Never build multiblocks across chunk borders. They will break when chunks unload.
The SILEX machine reprocesses depleted nuclear fuel. The default Fuel Reprocessor is disabled for most fuels.
SILEX recipes need Nitric Acid and a Laser Crystal. CO2 crystals work for LEU fuel, Bismuth for HEU.
Craft Nitric Acid in the Catalytic Chemistry Reactor: Nitrogen to Nitric Oxide to NO2 to Nitric Acid.
All Chemistry Reactor recipes require a Catalyst, crafted in the NC Assembler.
HBM and NuclearCraft share plates. The NC Pressurizer outputs HBM plates for Iron, Gold, Titanium, Copper, Aluminum, and Steel.
Steam Condensers (Mk I-III) convert exhaust steam back into water. Build one early if you use turbines.
Basic Plating, Solenoids, and Electric Motors are crafted at the HBM Anvil, not in a crafting table.
The Decay Hastener is an endgame machine. It requires Solinium nuggets and Euphemium to craft.
Solinium fuels come in three enrichment tiers: HES, MES, and LES. They are made from HBM Solinium billets.
Rocket engines and fuel tanks use HBM thrusters and fuselages. Check JEI for the crafting recipes.
The Warp Core requires Schrabidium blocks. Save your Schrabidium for space travel.
Craft radiation protection early: Iodine Pills, FNZP Tablets, and Radaway are all available.
For severe radiation exposure, use Strong Radaway or Radaway Flush.
Speed and Energy Upgrades for NuclearCraft machines require the Researcher machine.
Jetpacks start at Copper tier. The Wood and Stone tiers are disabled in this pack.
If something feels too tedious, you are probably doing it wrong. Check JEI for alternative recipes.
Never leave a reactor running unattended. Check your coolant levels and neutron flux regularly.
Connect with other players on the official Discord server, linked from the Main Menu.
